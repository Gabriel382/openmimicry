## Voice references

Voice recordings and cloned-voice profiles can be biometric and copyrighted
material. OpenMimicry stores them privately, requires an explicit consent
record for cloning/export paths, and excludes them from release artifacts.
Users must have the speaker's permission and comply with the selected provider,
model, and jurisdictional requirements.
